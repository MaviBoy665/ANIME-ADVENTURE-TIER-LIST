AA Value Tier List

Unofficial value list, currently updated. Use it as a guide only, not as the basis for every trade.

Each card shows the value, then demand (1–10, shown with a percent, red is low and green is high) and the trend.
General shows every role. DPS shows damage units plus both-role units, and Support shows support units plus both-role units. Order inside a tier is not a ranking. Based on Destructoid’s Update 21 list (Nov 4, 2026), with role notes from other guides. Soaring enemies can only be hit by Hill or Hybrid units standing on a hill.
Tap a card to see details. Search checks every section. Data is from the Bloxodes Anime Adventures wiki (Oct 30, 2026), so Update 21 additions may be missing.
Values come from the Trade values tab. Pick the exact entry (shiny and non-shiny are listed separately). Units with no number, like Owner’s choice, can’t be added. A trade within about 5% counts as fair.
